## What we do

Install Agent productizes AI automations for small and mid-size businesses.
We build reliable, reusable automations — primarily
[n8n](https://n8n.io) workflows wrapped with [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) —
and deploy them per client with **technician-led setup sessions**. No
"here's your login, good luck": a technician installs the automation in your
business, connects it to your tools, and stays until it works.

## How we build

A few principles run through everything in this org:

- **Automations are a product, not a project.** Workflows are versioned,
  reusable JSON — built once, installed many times, improved for everyone.
- **Reliability over cleverness.** Idempotent triggers, defensive parsing,
  explicit logging. An automation that silently fails is worse than no
  automation.
- **No secrets, no customer data in code.** Credentials live in vaults, never
  in git. Client data stays in client systems.
- **Built to be worked on by humans *and* AI agents.** Every repo carries
  agent-readable conventions so contributors — human or AI — are productive
  in their first hour.
